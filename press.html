About

Hatch one egg on your desktop and raise the little pet inside. Pet him until his eyes close, dress him in hats, give him a bed, and send him to sleep over at a friend's PC while you peek in. He learns from the way you live, and a month later he's like no other pet on earth.

What he does

  • Each egg hatches a different creature.
  • Pet him and watch his eyes close.
  • He gets better at playing ball the more you play.
  • Send him to a friend's PC for the night. You can still watch him play from yours.
  • Open his Den and try on hats, beds and toys. Whatever you pick, he wears on your desktop too.
  • Stay up late often and he learns to stay up with you.
