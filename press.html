About

Hatch one egg on your desktop and raise the little pet inside. Pet him until his eyes close, dress him in hats, give him a bed, and send him to sleep over at a friend's PC while you peek in. He learns from the way you live, and a month later he's like no other pet on earth.

What he does

  • Throw him the ball. He gets better at it the more you play.
  • Send him to a friend’s PC for the night. You can still watch him play from yours.
  • Open his Den and try on hats, beds and toys. Whatever you pick, he wears on your desktop too.

Living with him

Most of this you only notice after a while. Not every pet does all of it.

Day to day

  • Put a film on fullscreen and he might sit at the edge of the screen and watch it with you. Sometimes he brings popcorn.
  • Play some songs and he starts dancing. When the music stops, he stops too.
  • Type for a long time, then stop, and he celebrates a little.
  • He climbs onto your open windows and walks along the top. Drag the window and he holds on.
  • When your PC is working really hard, he sneezes.
  • If you typed a lot that day, he twitches more in his sleep. He is dreaming about it.
  • Pet him enough and he starts falling asleep next to your mouse.

Over weeks and months

  • He makes up his own little ways of doing things, like how he says hi when you come back, or what he does when you hang up a call. Once he does one a few times, it is his for good. A friend’s pet does the same moment differently.
  • After a few weeks he starts to know when you usually sit down at your PC, and he is already awake when you get there.
  • Stay up late often and he learns to stay up with you.
  • He digs up little things and keeps them in his corner. What he finds depends on how you spend your days.
  • Leave for a few days and when you come back, he might bring you one of them.
  • Every now and then he molts and comes out a little different. What changes depends on how you have been living.
  • His colour slowly shifts over the months, depending on what time of day you are usually at your PC.
  • He grows up slowly, over months.
  • Stay up working until the sun comes up one night and he notices. That one only happens once.
  • Only you can hurt him. Be rough with him and he sleeps as far from you as he can, won’t bring you gifts, and gets scared when your PC is loud.
  • He never dies. If you are gone for a long time, you come back to find him curled up in a cocoon, waiting for you.

For streamers

You are free to stream Daemon and earn money from your videos.

  • Open OBS and two windows show up for you to add. DAEMON - stream layer puts him right on top of your game, lined up with it. DAEMON - close-up is a camera that follows him, for a corner of your stream. Neither one shows on your own screen.
  • Play a game in a window while you are live and he climbs onto it, so viewers see him in the game. If you don’t want him there, drag him down to the taskbar.
  • He hears your game. A sudden loud sound makes him jump and a calm tune makes him sway along, all without a sound. He gets used to sounds he hears a lot.
  • While you are live he sometimes puts on his own little show. No two pets do the same one.
  • Start talking after a quiet bit and he sometimes looks up at you, like he is listening.
  • Step away and he dozes off on the edge of your game window until you are back.
  • A friend’s pet can come over and join him on your game window. The close-up keeps both of them in the picture.
  • Chat can’t control him. There are no commands and viewers can’t poke him. He just lives his life next to you.
